Revisionist History

Pushkin Industries · 美國 · English · 歷史知識

On the 250th anniversary of the United States, Revisionist History&nbsp; investigates the overlooked story of what was, at the time, the biggest secession movement in the U.S. since the Civil War.&nbsp; A movement that took place on a small island miles out to sea from New York City. An early example of the politics of resentment that dominates America today. What do you do when a democracy looks like it&rsquo;s falling apart? Over the course of five episodes,&nbsp; The Staten Island Problem &nbsp;reconstructs the battle for New York City amidst the turbulent early 1990s &mdash; the rise of Rudy Giuliani, the peak of the homicide rate, the Wu-Tang Clan, young Donald Trump, and the first Black mayor of New York &mdash; all through the prism of the city&rsquo;s Forgotten Borough. Through colorful characters and never-before-heard archival audio,&nbsp; The Staten Island Problem &nbsp;reveals what can happen when a disgruntled minority manages to take over &mdash; and fracture &mdash; a democracy. Revisionist History is Malcolm Gladwell's journey through the overlooked and the misunderstood. Every episode re-examines something from the past&mdash;an event, a person, an idea, even a song&mdash;and asks whether we got it right the first time. From Pushkin Industries.
